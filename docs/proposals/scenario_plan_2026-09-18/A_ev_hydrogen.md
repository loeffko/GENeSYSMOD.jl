## S2.6 Policy-conditionality flags (for the "upside if 45V/offtake reignite" framing)

- Everything electrolytic in the US is 45V-conditional: EIA AEO2026 projects "very little" electrolytic H2 in all cases once 45V construction window closes 2027-12-31; the two all-electrolysis hubs (ARCHES, PNWH2) are terminated; retained hubs are predominantly gas+CCS (45Q, which OBBBA left intact and extended to 2033 start).
- Upside triggers to model: (i) 45V begin-construction extension or safe-harbouring of the ~3.8 GW DOE-tracked firm pipeline (TX 1.6 GW + 1.25 GW Corpus Christi, IL 0.32 GW, UT 0.22 GW, NY 0.2 GW) -> at 55.5 kWh/kg and 80% CF, 3.8 GW = ~27 TWh/yr, ~0.48 Mt H2/yr; (ii) DOE strategy 10 Mt (2030) is 20x the committed pipeline; (iii) Canada's Atlantic export projects (CER: ~1.45 Mt electrolytic by 2040 = ~70-75 TWh) depend on H2Global/EU offtake and NL land access.
- EV upside is EPA-standard/ACC II-conditional: AEO2026 with standards ~53% BEV sales by 2032 vs ~20% by 2050 without; ACC II unenforceable pending California v. United States; Canada moved from a 100%-by-2035 mandate to non-binding 75%/90% targets with rebates.

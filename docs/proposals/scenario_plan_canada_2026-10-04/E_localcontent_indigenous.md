## S4 - Proposed variants

How the numbers are derived (all steps below are OWN ASSUMPTIONS built on the cited rows):

- **Share of new capacity that is partnership-gated.** Every recent utility procurement of new renewables in BC (2024 and 2025 calls), Ontario (LT2 W1: 14 of 14), Quebec (Hydro-Quebec wind strategy) and Manitoba (600 MW call) ended with 50-51 % or majority Indigenous ownership, and new Ontario transmission is 50 % First Nations by Hydro One policy. Alberta (merchant market) has no rule. OWN ASSUMPTION: 70 % of Canada's new wind/solar/hydro/nuclear capacity in the model (the share built under utility procurement in BC, ON, QC, MB, SK and the Atlantic provinces) is gated; 30 % (mainly Alberta merchant and behind-the-fence) is not.
- **Project-level WACC effect.** The 50-150 bp (RBC) and 150-250 bp (FNMPC) figures apply to the Indigenous partner's equity loan, not to the project. With a 50 % Indigenous share of an equity layer that is 30-40 % of the capital stack (OWN ASSUMPTION), the guaranteed slice is 15-20 % of project capital, so the project-level effect is 15-20 % x 50-250 bp = **8-50 bp**; central 20 % x 125 bp = **25 bp**. A risk premium for un-partnered projects has no quantitative source (NOT FOUND) and is set by assumption.
- **Timing.** Documented acceleration with partnership: 1-2 years (BC EA exemption), up to 3 years (federal 5+ to 2 years). Documented delay without consent: 10 months (Trans Mountain re-consultation), 13 years (Niagara line), 15 years and cancellation (OPG DGR).

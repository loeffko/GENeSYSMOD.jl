# Research D: wind re-acceleration and critical minerals (Canada scenarios 7, 8)

Research date: 2026-10-04 (S7, S8 signal check) and 2026-10-05 (S8 anchors, variants, gaps; second session with web search). STATUS: complete (S7 and S8). S8 gaps are listed explicitly at the end.

Conventions
- "snippet - verify" = figure seen only in a search-result snippet; the page itself was not fetched or was not readable.
- "ASSUMPTION" = my own number, not taken from a source.
- "LOCAL CER FILE" = `GENeSYS-MOD.data_francesca/NA_inputs/canada_power_ef2026.xlsx`, the project's own consolidated copy of the Canada Energy Regulator open data for "Canada's Energy Future 2026" (published 2026-03-17, policy cut-off November 2025; https://doi.org/10.35002/rd69-q158). Numbers were read from that workbook, not from the CER website.
- Currency: 1 CAD = 0.685 EUR. Derivation: Bank of Canada annual average 2023, USD/CAD 1.3497 and EUR/CAD 1.4597 (https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/annual-average-exchange-rates/), which agrees with the project rate 1 USD = 0.925 EUR (0.925 / 1.3497 = 0.685). For reference the 2025 average was USD/CAD 1.3978, EUR/CAD 1.5782 (1 CAD = 0.634 EUR at 2025 market rates). No deflation between C$2024/C$2025 and EUR2023 was applied; converted values are therefore approximate (a few percent high).

## S8 - Proposed variants

Model lever: TWh ADDED to the Canada "general" load (busbar), which is flat in the frozen base (513 / 518 / 522 / 525 TWh in 2025 / 2030 / 2035 / 2040). Canada is one node, so the provincial rows are composition only.

Conventions for every number in this section (all are my own arithmetic from the anchors above):
- TWh = MW x load factor x 8,760 h. Load factor ASSUMPTION: 85 % for mines and concentrators, 90 % for potash, smelters and chemical plants. Site demand is taken as busbar demand (no loss adjustment, ASSUMPTION).
- Increments are relative to 2025. Projects already running in 2025 are not counted.
- OFF-GRID projects (Rook I, Eagle's Nest, Strange Lake, the territories) are zero unless a variant says they are connected.
- Gold-only projects (e.g. Eskay Creek, 21 MW) and steel electric-arc furnaces (e.g. Algoma, about 280 MW) are left out: real load growth, but not part of this signal.
- Battery CELL plants are shown as a separate optional add-on, because they are manufacturing rather than minerals and their MW were not found.
- These loads run almost flat through the year. If the increment is added to the general load with the general load shape, its peak contribution is slightly overstated; a flat profile is the better representation.
